**Description**

Better Solitaire is Klondike with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Choose draw one or draw three, then play by dragging cards or tapping a card to send it to a legal destination. Set the deal mix anywhere from random to verified winnable deals.

Choose the table that suits you. Classic uses green felt, clean typography, and familiar illustrated court cards. Vintage pairs burgundy felt with antique-inspired cards and letterpress details. You can switch styles without restarting your deal.

Features:

- Draw one and draw three
- Random and verified winnable deals
- Unlimited stock passes
- Hints and undo
- Optional automatic foundation moves
- Restart the same deal
- Daily streaks and personal records
- Classic green and Vintage burgundy card-table styles
- Sound and haptic feedback controls
- Full offline play
- Game state saved on your device

Better Solitaire has no accounts and collects no data. Close the app at any time and return to the same deal.
